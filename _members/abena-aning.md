Abena is a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Baylor University. She holds a Bachelor's degrees in Electrical Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. Abena's research focuses on explainable AI for root-cause diagnosis of learning-enabled autonomous systems and cyber-physical systems. Her current work focuses on the investigation of causal inference, spatiotemporal graph learning, and symbolic reasoning for identifying and explaining failures in autonomous systems. Her research aims to distinguish root causes from propagated faults across interconnected sensing, perception, planning, and control components.
