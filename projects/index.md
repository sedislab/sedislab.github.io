## Research Projects
<h4>Formal assurance of Learning-Enabled Autonomous Systems</h4>
<br/><img src="images/fm_research1.jpg" class="float-right" alt="Formal methods for robotics and CPS"/>
<p>
Learning-enabled autonomous systems increasingly rely on artificial intelligence and machine learning to perceive their environments, make decisions, and perform complex tasks. However, the unpredictable behavior of learned models, environmental uncertainties, and adversarial conditions make it difficult to guarantee their safety, security, and reliability using conventional testing methods.
</p>
<p>
This research develops formal methods and computational frameworks for specifying, verifying, and assuring the behavior of learning-enabled autonomous systems. We investigate temporal logic and hyperproperty-based techniques to capture safety, security, and performance requirements, including properties involving multiple system executions. Our work combines formal verification, runtime monitoring, and learning-based control to detect specification violations, assess system behavior, and support safe decision-making under uncertainty.
</p>
